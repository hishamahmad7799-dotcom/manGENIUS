Privacy Policy

How ManGENIUS Staffing LLC collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you visit our website or contact us about our staffing and recruitment services.

Effective Date: [COMPANY TO CONFIRM]
Entity: ManGENIUS Staffing LLC

ManGENIUS Staffing LLC ("ManGENIUS," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website or contact us about our staffing and recruitment services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect:

  • Contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Employment-related information, including your resume, work experience, skills, education, qualifications, and professional interests.
  • Employer enquiry information, including organization name, contact details, staffing requirements, and messages.
  • Information you voluntarily provide through website forms.
  • Technical information, such as browser type, device information, and website usage data, where applicable.

Please do not submit Social Security numbers, financial account information, or unnecessary sensitive personal or medical information through general website forms or resume uploads.

2. How We Use Information

We may use submitted information to:

  • Respond to employer and general enquiries.
  • Review candidate resumes and professional qualifications.
  • Identify potential employment or recruitment opportunities.
  • Connect candidates with prospective employers when appropriate.
  • Communicate about relevant staffing opportunities and recruitment enquiries.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website and services.
  • Protect the website and address security or misuse concerns.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Resume and Candidate Information

When you submit a resume or candidate enquiry, ManGENIUS may review your information for recruitment-related purposes.

Candidate information may be shared with prospective employers when relevant to a potential opportunity and subject to applicable requirements and appropriate authorization.

Submitting a resume does not guarantee an interview, referral, employment offer, or job placement.

Candidates should ensure that the information they provide is accurate and that they are authorized to share the information contained in their resume.

4. Information Sharing

We may share information where reasonably necessary with:

  • Prospective employers for relevant recruitment opportunities, subject to applicable requirements and appropriate candidate authorization.
  • Website hosting, form processing, file storage, and other service providers that support our operations.
  • Government authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and safety.

We do not describe candidate information as publicly available or freely accessible to employers. Access should be limited to authorized individuals with a legitimate business need.
